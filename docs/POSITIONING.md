# UXcalibur positioning and design direction

Owner direction, 2026-10-06. Local reviewable revision; live site and npm 0.1.0 remain the published baseline.

**Turn rough apps into exceptional experiences.**

UXcalibur gives your coding agent an expert UX method to assess an app, design a coherent upgrade, and carry that design through implementation. It works across product structure, user journeys, interactions, visual design, language, accessibility, and the details of real use.

**Draw out your app's potential.** The sword-from-the-stone story is about elevation: an app or codebase can become an exceptional experience when its potential is recognized and expertly developed. The sword-in-code mark connects the story to the actual input.

**Shape. Refine. Hone.** The blade can be wielded for broad structural changes, thoughtful refinements, and precision polish. These are scales of work, not mandatory phases. A whole-app request receives whole-app inspection; a focused request receives the same depth at its chosen scale.

## The human-guided pass

The owner reviewed UXcalibur's own UX and found that “help people accomplish more” did not explain the product category or capability. “Few justified changes” made the tool sound smaller than intended. Leading with focus/exclusions exposed process details before visitors understood the promise.

The proposed correction makes the transformation concrete, teaches the range of work, and moves optional controls into usage guidance. It changes the skill's behavior as well as the public explanation.

This review also demonstrates why conceptual models belong in UX work. Names, language, metaphors, and onboarding shape how people understand and wield an app. A metaphor should map to real capability and scale; human feedback can reveal where that mapping breaks.

## What changes in the method

- Broad app reviews cover meaningful surfaces and core journeys, with a coherent target experience and prioritized transformation plan.
- Shape/refine/hone guides scale without forcing a redesign or imposing a small finding quota.
- Visual craft and conceptual framing receive explicit attention alongside interaction and implementation contracts.
- Detailed specs retain executable design, supporting contracts, acceptance coverage, and progress tracking.
- Authorized implementation includes rendered design review and journey verification.

Keep useful architecture, data, evidence traceability, supplied scope, and authorization boundaries. These remain implementation discipline rather than the headline proposition.

## Quality ambition and proof

“Exceptional” and “world-class” describe the ambition. Define it through coherent structure, intuitive journeys, deliberate composition, readable language, accessible interaction, honest feedback, and reliable execution. They are not a guarantee conferred by installation.

The existing synthetic example remains a precision-honing proof. It does not establish that the method has already delivered a world-class whole-app transformation. The positioning pass is human design feedback, not measured user research.

## Release boundary

The revised source, documentation, artwork, and local FilePress preview are prepared for review. No new npm version, public push, or deployment is part of this positioning pass. Published 0.1.0 remains available; release packaging/versioning and live publication follow after this direction is settled. SaaS remains deferred.
