<div class="section-rule"><span>01 / Wield it at any scale</span><span>Broad strokes → precision honing</span></div>

<div class="steps-grid">
<section><p class="step-number">01</p><h2>Shape.</h2><p>Rethink the product's structure, navigation, mental model, and core journeys. Give the whole experience a clear, coherent direction.</p></section>
<section><p class="step-number">02</p><h2>Refine.</h2><p>Develop intuitive interactions and deliberate visual design. Bring hierarchy, typography, layout, content, and responsive behavior into harmony.</p></section>
<section><p class="step-number">03</p><h2>Hone.</h2><p>Polish language, focus, feedback, states, and finishing details. Make each moment feel considered, from first use to the final action.</p></section>
</div>

Start with the whole app or choose the part you want to improve. UXcalibur meets the work at its scale, from a bold transformation to a precise finishing pass.
