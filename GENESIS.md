# UXcalibur

Created 2026-10-05. Current product direction updated 2026-10-06 from a human-guided UXcalibur pass on UXcalibur itself.

**Turn rough apps into exceptional experiences.**

UXcalibur gives a coding agent an expert UX method to inspect an existing app or codebase, recognize its design potential, design a coherent upgrade, and carry it through implementation when requested. The ambition is an expertly designed and executed experience, from the overall product model to the finishing details.

## Draw out the potential

The sword-from-the-stone story is about elevation. Drawing UXcalibur brings an app's potential into reach. Keep the spelling **UXcalibur**, ending in **-ur** like Excalibur.

Make the metaphor concrete: the mark is a sword framed by code brackets, and the larger illustration draws the sword from a stone shaped like a code block. The story should help people understand what the product does and how to wield it.

## A blade for every scale

- **Shape:** product concepts and mental models, information architecture, navigation, core journeys, and substantial layout or capability changes.
- **Refine:** interactions, visual hierarchy, typography, spacing, language, responsive behavior, and accessibility.
- **Hone:** precise wording, focus, feedback, states, alignment, transitions, and continuity.

These are scales of work, not a mandatory three-stage process. An early prototype may need a transformation; a mature app may need a targeted refinement or precision polish. Users can specify an aspect to focus on or preserve. Those controls support the assignment without defining the headline proposition.

For a broad app request, inspect the meaningful surfaces and core journeys. Do not silently shrink it to one flow or a few findings. For a focused request, apply the same design depth at its stated scale.

## What expert UX work includes

Understand the app's purpose and audience. Assess structure, journeys, interactions, visual craft, language, responsive/accessibility behavior, and states/recovery. Evaluate the mental model communicated by names, metaphors, navigation, and onboarding.

Show a concrete target experience and prioritized changes that work together. Make product and visual decisions specific enough to build. A list of heuristic issues or a cosmetic theme swap is inadequate when the product needs a coherent redesign.

Connect interface promises to actual code and supporting contracts. Preserve useful architecture, user data, and unrelated work. During implementation, inspect rendered screens and verify real journeys. A passing build alone does not establish design quality, and a predicted usability gain is not a measured result.

“World-class” describes the quality ambition. Give it substance through coherent structure, intuitive journeys, deliberate visual design, accessible interaction, reliable recovery, and execution quality.

## Delivery forms

The first product is the versioned skill at [skills/uxcalibur](skills/uxcalibur/SKILL.md) with an npm installer. The agent supplies the model, tools, app access, and execution environment.

An app review produces an assessment, target design, and prioritized upgrade path. A focused refinement or polish pass addresses the requested part. A detailed spec resolves flow/state/data/visual contracts, implementation packets, acceptance scenarios, and a living tracker. Authorized implementation carries that design into reviewable code.

The method originated in a detailed UX pass on DictaWhisper. It retains that implementation depth while adapting to different products and scales. Private application content remains outside public examples.

## Developer launch and current revision

The bounded developer launch is complete: MIT, Codex/Claude Code/Cursor installation, npm **uxcalibur@0.1.0**, public GitHub, and a FilePress developer site at [uxcalibur-dev.pages.dev](https://uxcalibur-dev.pages.dev). The owner connects **uxcalibur.dev** through Cloudflare Pages.

This positioning and method revision is a local next-release candidate. It has not changed the published 0.1.0 package or live site. The earlier locked briefs and proof receipts remain historical release evidence; this direction supersedes their focused-default positioning for future work.

**SaaS and the operated service remain deferred.** The developer product has no account system, billing service, model runtime, or automatic audit command. Future forms are separate decisions.

## Development and evidence

Use ForgeTrail and AppLedger for decisions, phase state, work, verification, and handoffs. FilePress is the established site engine. Keep the skill self-contained; do not ship the proof harness or project-memory tools as product runtime dependencies.

The synthetic review-inbox proof demonstrates one precise interaction change: return restores the originating row, focus, query, and scroll. The second recommended change remains unimplemented. This establishes actionability on that fixture, not a whole-app transformation or measured customer efficacy.

The owner's human-guided review of UXcalibur demonstrates another UX dimension: reframing the product's value proposition and metaphor so people understand its capability and range. Record it as design feedback and a proposed correction, not a usability study.
